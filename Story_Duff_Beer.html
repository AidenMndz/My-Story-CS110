01 / OUR STORY

It started with
a wrong turn.

A mysterious map. An old arcade. Our names already on the scoreboard.

We found the map inside an arcade machine, with a star outside town and two words: “Find us.” We packed snacks, piled into the van, and promptly missed the exit.

That wrong turn brought us to an abandoned amusement park. One arcade was still glowing. Beside it sat a red-and-cream Duff can with a note tucked underneath. Someone had been expecting us.

Now you’re part of the crew. Look around the can and reveal the next clue.
